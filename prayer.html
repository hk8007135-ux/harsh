ST. PAUL'S SR. SEC. SCHOOL, ETAH

School Prayers

Morning Prayer

Heavenly Father
Hear our prayer
Keep us
in Thy Loving care
guard us
through the live long day
in our work
and in our play
keep us pure
kind and true
in everything
we say and do.

Our Father
who art in heaven
hallowed be Thy name
Thy kingdom come
Thy will be done
on earth
as it is
in Heaven
Give us this day
our daily bread
and forgive us
our sins
as we forgive
those who sin
against us
And lead us not
into temptation
but deliver us
from evil
for Thine is the kingdom
power
and Glory
Forever and Ever.

- Amen


Prayer Before Lunch

Bless us O Lord!
And these Thy gifts, which we are
About to receive, from Thy bounty.

Bless the poor and the hungry.

Thank you Lord!!

- Amen


Prayer After Classes

We give you Thanks Almighty God
for all the Benefits we have
received from Thy bounty
who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

-- Amen
