Morning Prayer

Heavenly Father / Hear our prayer / Keep us / in Thy Loving care / guard us / through the live long day / in our work /
and in our play/ keep us pure / kind and true / in everything / we say and do.
Our Father / who art in heaven / hallowed be Thy name / Thy kingdom come / Thy will be done /
on earth / as it is / in Heaven / Give us this day / our daily bread / and forgive us / our sins / as we forgave /
those who sin / against us / And lead us not / into temptation ? but deliver us / from evil /
for Thine is the kingdom / power / and Glory / Forever and Ever.

- Amen
