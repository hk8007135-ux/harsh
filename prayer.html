School Prayer

Morning Prayer

Lord, make me an instrument of your peace.
Where there is hatred, let me sow love;
Where there is injury, pardon;
Where there is doubt, faith;
Where there is despair, hope;
Where there is darkness, light;
And where there is sadness, joy.

Prayer for Wisdom & Learning

O God, source of all knowledge and truth,
Bless our studies and enlighten our minds.
Grant us wisdom to learn with enthusiasm,
Courage to stand for what is right,
And kindness to help our fellow students every day.
