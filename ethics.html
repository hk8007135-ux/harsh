Key Cyber Safety Guidelines

  • Verify Before You Click: Avoid clicking on suspicious discount links or unknown attachments.
  • Protect Financial Details: Never enter card details, CVV, or OTPs on unverified websites.
  • Report Cyber Fraud Immediately: Call National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Consult Before Acting: Always inform parents, guardians, or trusted adults before making online transactions.
